# Solvren Executive Guide

A guide for CEOs, CFOs, revenue operations leadership, engineering leadership, and risk executives.

Last updated: 2026-06-17

---

## 1. What Solvren Solves

Solvren shows what revenue is at risk, what needs action, and what value was protected.

It addresses three executive problems:

- revenue-impacting systems fail quietly
- risky changes move without enough business context
- teams struggle to prove what was protected after the work is done

---

## 2. The Executive View

Executives do not need a separate interface. Solvren uses one universal app model:

| Area | Executive question |
|------|--------------------|
| **Home** | What matters right now? |
| **Decisions** | What needs approval, proof, or escalation? |
| **Problems** | What is putting revenue at risk? |
| **Proof** | What value did Solvren help protect? |
| **Setup** | Are the right systems, people, and controls in place? |

---

## 3. What To Look At First

Start with **Home**.

Home should answer within seconds:

- How much money is at risk?
- What needs attention?
- What problems did Solvren detect?
- What value has been protected?
- Is setup coverage healthy?

Then use **Decisions** for the short list of work that needs a person to act.

---

## 4. Decisions And Change Risk

Solvren decision pages are designed to be understandable without reading implementation details.

They answer:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- What should happen next?
- Who owns it?
- What proof exists?

Technical logs, source signals, scoring details, and audit records remain available in details sections for operators and auditors.

---

## 5. Proof And Value

Use **Proof** when you need to show business value.

Proof includes:

- revenue protected
- recovered revenue
- avoided loss
- risks prevented
- decisions accelerated
- incidents avoided
- value stories
- board-ready proof packets

Proof packets summarize business context, decision status, proof, approvals, delivery history, and outcomes.

---

## 6. Enterprise Trust

Solvren supports enterprise trust through:

- restricted access
- customer-controlled support access
- SSO
- audit logs
- approval traceability
- proof requirements
- safe automation controls
- license and rollout scope

The product is designed to hide unnecessary complexity from everyday users while keeping full records available for review.

---

## 7. Operational Benefits

| Outcome | How Solvren Helps |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Protect revenue** | Finds and prioritizes problems before they become expensive. |
| **Accelerate decisions** | Shows owners, proof, risk, and the next action in one place. |
| **Reduce incident risk** | Requires proof and approval for sensitive revenue changes. |
| **Improve accountability** | Makes ownership, decisions, and audit history visible. |
| **Prove ROI** | Converts outcomes into value stories and proof packets. |
