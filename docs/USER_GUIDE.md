# Solvren User Guide

A guide for everyday users: executives, finance leaders, engineering leaders, submitters, reviewers, RevOps, risk managers, and operations staff.

Last updated: 2026-06-17

---

## 1. What Solvren Does

Solvren shows what revenue is at risk, what needs action, and what value was protected.

It helps teams:

- find revenue-impacting problems
- review risky changes before they ship
- route work to the right owner
- collect the proof needed for confident decisions
- verify outcomes and prove protected value

---

## 2. Current Product Model

The logged-in app uses one universal interface for every role.

| Area | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Home** | Money at risk, urgent work, problems detected, protected value, and setup health. |
| **Decisions** | Approvals, assigned problems, missing proof, blocked work, overdue work, and system follow-up. |
| **Problems** | Revenue-impacting issues Solvren found across connected systems. |
| **Proof** | Protected value, recovered revenue, avoided loss, value stories, reports, and proof packets. |
| **Setup** | Connected systems, users, decision rules, security, license, and organization settings. |

Older docs may reference Dashboard, Reviews, Impact, Outcomes, Governance, Evidence, or Signals as primary surfaces. Those capabilities still exist, but the main product language is now Home, Decisions, Problems, Proof, and Setup.

---

## 3. Home

Home is the first place to understand why Solvren matters today.

It summarizes:

- money at risk
- items needing action
- problems detected
- value protected
- setup and coverage health

---

## 4. Decisions

Decisions is the short list of work that needs a person to act.

It can include:

- approvals waiting for you
- assigned problems
- changes missing proof
- overdue or blocked work
- system tasks that need follow-up

Each item should answer what it is, why it matters, and what happens next.

---

## 5. Problems

Problems are revenue-impacting issues Solvren finds across connected systems.

Examples:

- billing mismatch
- failed renewal workflow
- CRM routing issue
- integration drift
- automation failure
- data issue affecting revenue operations

Open a Problem to understand the business impact, owner, next action, related systems, and proof.

---

## 6. Change Reviews

Revenue-sensitive changes have a decision page built for both leaders and operators.

The page answers:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- What should happen next?
- Who owns it?
- What proof exists?

Technical logs, source signals, scoring details, and audit records remain available in details sections.

---

## 7. Proof

Proof is the value story.

It includes:

- revenue protected
- recovered revenue
- avoided loss
- risks prevented
- decisions accelerated
- incidents avoided
- value stories
- proof packets

Use Proof when a CEO, board member, auditor, or customer stakeholder asks what Solvren protected.

---

## 8. Profiles And Avatars

Users can upload a profile picture and set a display name from Setup.

Avatars are resized for consistent display and appear beside people in:

- the topbar user menu
- team management
- Decisions
- approval ownership areas

This makes ownership and assignments easier to scan.

---

## 9. Search, Notifications, And Audit

Use topbar search to find problems, decisions, proof, setup pages, and historical records.

Notifications appear for approvals, blockers, missing proof, overdue work, and important updates.

Important actions are recorded for auditability, including decisions, proof changes, access grants, configuration changes, and integration activity.
