# 14-Day Revenue Protection Pilot Plan

## Pilot Promise

In 14 days, Solvren helps you connect one revenue workflow, detect your first revenue risk, and produce an executive-ready proof packet.

## Best Fit

- B2B SaaS or subscription business
- Stripe, Salesforce, HubSpot, NetSuite, Chargebee, or similar revenue systems
- Complex pricing, billing, renewals, refunds, or revenue operations
- Finance and engineering both touch revenue-impacting change

## Day 1: Connect Systems

Choose one workflow and connect the minimum systems needed to observe signals. Recommended starting points:

- Stripe and HubSpot
- Stripe and Salesforce
- Billing plus CRM
- Pricing change workflow

## Day 2: Choose What To Protect

Pick one risk surface:

- Failed payment recovery
- Pricing drift
- CRM/billing mismatch
- Refund leakage
- Risky billing or pricing change awaiting approval

## Day 3: Detect First Risks

Run the first scan, review detected issues, and confirm whether the findings match real operational risk.

## Day 5: Review First Proof Packet

Review revenue at risk, missing proof, ownership, and recommended next actions with Finance, RevOps, and Engineering.

## Days 6-10: Tune and Act

Adjust assumptions, route owners, add proof, and decide whether any workflow should move from monitoring to action.

## Days 11-14: Prove Value

Produce an executive summary showing what revenue was at risk, what changed, what was prevented or resolved, and where Solvren should expand next.

## Success Criteria

- One connected revenue workflow
- One validated risk or decision
- One executive proof packet
- Clear expansion recommendation
