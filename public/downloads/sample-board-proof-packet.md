Solvren helped the organization identify, review, and control revenue-sensitive operational work before it created financial or customer impact.

## What Happened

A pricing rule change touched billing, CRM, and renewal workflows. Solvren identified the decision as revenue-sensitive, required proof, routed the right reviewers, and kept the decision from moving forward until evidence was complete.

## Why It Mattered

The change could have created underbilling, invoice disputes, renewal delay, and manual finance correction. Solvren made the risk visible before the change shipped.

## Controls Used

- Required proof checklist
- Finance and RevOps approval routing
- Revenue exposure estimate
- Audit trail
- Board-ready export

## Outcome

The team completed proof, clarified rollback ownership, and approved the change with documented evidence. Leaders had a clear record of what was protected and why the decision was safe.

## Board Takeaway

Solvren creates a repeatable revenue protection layer around operational changes that otherwise depend on informal coordination, manual review, and memory.
