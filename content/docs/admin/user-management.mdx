## Overview

User management lives under **Setup -> Team & access**. It controls who can access Solvren, what they can do, and how they appear when work is assigned.

Proper user configuration helps Decisions, approvals, and Problems route to the right people.

---

## Inviting Users

Administrators can invite users to the organization.

To invite a user:

1. Open **Setup**.
2. Open **Team & access**.
3. Click **Invite user**.
4. Enter the user's email address.
5. Assign a role.

The user receives an invitation email with instructions for joining the organization.

---

## Profiles And Avatars

Users can manage their own display name and profile picture from Setup.

Profile pictures are resized for consistent display and appear throughout the app, including:

- the topbar user menu
- team member rows
- Decisions
- approval owner cards

Demo workspaces allow profile updates even though most operational writes remain restricted.

---

## User Roles

Roles determine what actions users can perform.

### Owner

Full organization access. Owners can configure organization settings, license scope, users, security, and decision rules.

### Admin

Administrators manage setup and configuration.

They can:

- invite users
- configure approval roles and mappings
- manage evidence and proof expectations
- manage integrations and setup
- review diagnostics and security settings

### Submitter

Submitters can create and edit changes and submit them for review.

### Reviewer

Reviewers evaluate and approve changes assigned to them.

### Viewer

Viewers can see permitted records but cannot manage organization settings.

---

## Managing User Access

Administrators can update user roles at any time.

They can also:

- remove users from the organization
- maintain approval role assignments
- grant explicit access to restricted records
- keep owner/admin access limited to people who need it

Removing unused accounts helps maintain security.

---

## Best Practices

Recommended practices include:

- Invite the decision makers who own revenue-sensitive workflows.
- Keep admins limited to people who actually manage setup.
- Assign reviewers for each important revenue area.
- Ask users to add profile pictures so ownership is easy to scan.
- Review access when people change roles or leave the company.
