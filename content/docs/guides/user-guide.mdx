## Overview

Solvren helps teams protect revenue and manage change. It gives every user the same simple product model:

- **Home** - what matters right now
- **Decisions** - what needs action
- **Problems** - what Solvren found
- **Proof** - what value was protected
- **Setup** - systems, people, rules, and controls

This guide explains how people use the app during normal work.

---

# Home

Home answers the executive question first: what revenue is at risk, what needs attention, what problems were detected, what value was protected, and whether setup coverage is healthy.

Use Home when you want the fastest read on why Solvren matters today.

---

# Decisions

Decisions is the short list of work that needs a person to act.

It can include:

- approvals waiting for you
- assigned problems
- changes missing proof
- overdue or blocked work
- system follow-up tasks

Each row points to the next action instead of forcing users to inspect raw workflow state.

---

# Problems

Problems are revenue-impacting issues Solvren found across connected systems.

Examples include:

- billing or invoice mismatches
- failed payment or renewal workflows
- CRM routing failures
- integration drift
- automation failures
- operational gaps that could affect customers or revenue

Open a problem to understand what happened, why it matters, who owns it, and what should happen next.

---

# Change Reviews

Revenue-sensitive changes have a decision page built for both leaders and operators.

Each page answers:

- What happened?
- Why does it matter?
- What should happen next?
- Who owns it?
- What proof exists?

Technical logs, source signals, scoring details, and audit history are still available in system detail sections.

---

# Proof

Proof is where Solvren shows value.

It includes:

- revenue protected
- recovered revenue
- avoided loss
- risks prevented
- decisions accelerated
- incidents avoided
- value stories
- board-ready proof packets

Use Proof when a leader, board member, auditor, or customer stakeholder asks what Solvren protected.

---

# Setup

Setup is where admins and owners manage:

- connected systems
- users and roles
- profile pictures and display names
- decision rules
- approval mappings
- evidence expectations
- security and data protection
- license scope
- diagnostics

Most users will only need a few Setup pages. Advanced controls remain available without cluttering everyday work.

---

# Profiles And Avatars

Users can upload a profile picture and set a display name from Setup. Solvren resizes profile pictures for display and uses them throughout the app so ownership is easier to recognize.

Avatars appear in places such as:

- the topbar user menu
- team and access management
- Decisions
- approval ownership areas

---

# Evidence And Proof

Solvren uses proof to help reviewers decide confidently.

Proof may include:

- PR or change diff
- test plan
- rollback plan
- monitoring plan
- revenue validation
- incident links
- delivery logs
- approval history

Missing proof blocks or slows decisions depending on configured rules.

---

# Notifications And Search

Solvren notifies users when work needs attention, approvals are pending, proof is missing, or decisions become overdue.

Use the top search bar to find problems, decisions, proof, setup pages, and historical records.

---

# Audit History

Important actions are recorded for accountability:

- change creation
- proof updates
- approval decisions
- access grants
- configuration changes
- integration and system events

Audit history supports trust, troubleshooting, and enterprise review.
