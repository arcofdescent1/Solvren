## The fastest path to value

Solvren is designed to make value obvious quickly: connect systems, choose what to protect, invite decision makers, and see the first risk or protected value.

---

## Step 1: Connect your systems

Start by integrating core systems such as Stripe, HubSpot, Salesforce, Jira, GitHub, NetSuite, or your data warehouse.

This enables Solvren to ingest signals and begin monitoring for problems.

**Guide:** [Connect your first system](/docs/get-started/connect-your-first-system)

---

## Step 2: Choose what to protect

Use **Setup** to confirm the revenue areas Solvren should watch first: billing, pricing, lead routing, renewals, revenue recognition, integrations, or other business-critical workflows.

---

## Step 3: Invite decision makers

Invite the people who should see and act on revenue-sensitive work. Typical first users include a finance leader, RevOps owner, engineering leader, admin, and at least one reviewer.

Each user can personalize their profile with a display name and avatar so assignments are easier to recognize.

**Guide:** [Set up your organization](/docs/get-started/setup)

---

## Step 4: Detect your first problem

Once connected, detector packs automatically identify issues such as:

- Failed payments
- Data inconsistencies
- Revenue mismatches

Each problem includes supporting details and linked source data.

**Guide:** [Detect your first issue](/docs/get-started/detect-your-first-issue)

---

## Step 5: Review your first decision

Use **Decisions** when Solvren needs a person to approve, add proof, follow up, or resolve assigned work.

Change review pages show:

- what happened
- why it matters
- what should happen next
- who owns it
- what proof exists

**Guide:** [Create your first change](/docs/get-started/first-change)

---

## Step 6: Verify results

Solvren verifies that the action succeeded and confirms the problem is resolved.

---

## Step 7: See your first value

Track:

- Recovered revenue
- Avoided loss
- Impact over time
- Decisions accelerated
- Board-ready proof packets

**Guide:** [View your first value](/docs/get-started/view-your-first-value)

---

## What to expect next

Solvren will begin:

- Detecting new problems continuously
- Recommending actions
- Building proof of protected value
- Improving coverage as more systems are connected
