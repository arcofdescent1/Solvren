# Solvren Documentation

## Protect revenue with a product people can understand quickly

Solvren shows what revenue is at risk, what needs action, and what value was protected. The logged-in product is organized around five stable areas:

- **Home** - money at risk, urgent work, problems detected, protected value, and setup health.
- **Decisions** - approvals, blocked work, missing proof, and the next clear action.
- **Problems** - revenue-impacting problems Solvren found across connected systems.
- **Proof** - protected value, outcomes, reports, ROI, and board-ready proof packets.
- **Setup** - connected systems, users, decision rules, security, license, and organization settings.

The deeper engine still detects, scores, routes, acts, verifies, and audits. The UI presents that complexity in business language first, with technical details available where operators and admins need them.

---

## How Solvren Works Now

1. **Connect systems**
   Solvren watches billing, CRM, finance, support, code, and operational systems.

2. **Find risk**
   Detectors and change reviews identify problems or risky decisions that could affect revenue.

3. **Decide what to do**
   Solvren shows who owns the work, what proof exists, what is missing, and the next action.

4. **Fix the problem**
   Teams resolve the problem directly or use governed playbooks and write-back workflows where enabled.

5. **Prove value**
   Verification, outcome records, value stories, and proof packets show what revenue was protected.
