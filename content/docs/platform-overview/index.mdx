## What is Solvren?

Solvren is a revenue protection and change management platform. It shows what revenue is at risk, what needs action, and what value was protected.

---

## The Product Model

The logged-in app uses one universal navigation model for every role:

| Area | What it answers |
|------|-----------------|
| Home | What matters right now? |
| Decisions | What needs approval, proof, or follow-up? |
| Problems | What revenue-impacting problems did Solvren find? |
| Proof | What value was protected, recovered, or prevented? |
| Setup | What systems, people, rules, and security controls are configured? |

Executives, finance leaders, engineering leaders, risk managers, and admins use the same interface. The content is prioritized by context, but Solvren does not split people into separate role-specific apps.

---

## The Closed Loop Underneath

Solvren still operates as a closed loop:

**Connect -> Find risk -> Decide -> Fix -> Prove**

---

## Core Capabilities

### Connected Systems

Connect billing, CRM, finance, support, code, and operational systems so Solvren can monitor real business workflows.

### Problems

Identify revenue-impacting problems from detector packs, integration data, and operational signals.

### Decisions

Review risky changes, missing proof, approvals, blockers, and ownership in one prioritized work surface.

### Protected Value

Track revenue at risk, recovered revenue, avoided loss, incidents prevented, and decisions accelerated.

### Proof

Generate proof packets, value stories, reports, and audit-ready records that leaders can review outside the operational workflow.

### Enterprise Controls

Manage users, roles, SSO, support access, restricted visibility, demo workspaces, license scope, audit logs, and safe automation policy.

---

## Why It Matters

Most systems show alerts. Solvren is designed to make the next business action obvious.

Solvren ensures issues are:

- Found
- Prioritized by revenue exposure
- Routed to the right owners
- Backed by proof
- Fixed or escalated
- Verified and measured

---

## Who It's For

- CEOs and executives who need the business answer quickly
- Finance and RevOps leaders who own revenue accuracy
- Engineering leaders who need safe change management
- Risk and compliance teams who need proof and auditability
- Admins who configure systems, rules, access, and enterprise controls

---

## Outcome

A platform that helps a company protect revenue, manage change elegantly, and prove the value of the work.
