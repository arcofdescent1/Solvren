## Why Proof Matters

Detection and action are not enough.

Leaders need to know:

- What revenue was at risk?
- What did Solvren help the team do?
- Was the outcome verified?
- What value was recovered or protected?
- Where is the evidence?

The **Proof** area answers those questions.

---

## What Proof Includes

Solvren brings together:

- revenue protected
- recovered revenue
- avoided loss
- decisions accelerated
- risks prevented
- incidents avoided
- value stories
- outcome reports
- board-ready proof packets

---

## Verification Process

After a problem is resolved or an action runs, Solvren can:

- query external systems
- confirm state changes
- validate outcomes
- attach evidence
- attribute recovered revenue or avoided loss

---

## Value Types

### Recovered Revenue

Directly regained revenue.

### Avoided Loss

Prevented revenue loss.

### Protected Value

Revenue exposure that was reduced, prevented, or controlled through a decision, proof requirement, action, or mitigation.

### Decisions Accelerated

Revenue-sensitive decisions that moved faster because the right proof, owner, and business context were visible.

---

## Proof Packets

Proof packets summarize business context, decision status, proof, approvals, delivery history, and outcomes so a CEO, board member, auditor, or customer stakeholder can review the story without reading operational details.

---

## Confidence

Each value event should make confidence visible. Value may be based on direct verification, supporting evidence, modeled estimates, or a combination of those sources.
